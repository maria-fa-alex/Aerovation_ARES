About us

Who we are

We are a group of sixth form students taking part in the Conrad Challenge. As individuals we bring different areas of expertise, and our specialist interests complement one another to form Aerovation.

5 team members Conrad Challenge Aviation & Aerospace

Our story

From classroom physics to real-world aviation

We are all in the same school year group and heard about the Conrad Challenge being offered at our school. Each of us thought this would be a fantastic opportunity to apply the science we learn in lessons to real-world applications — and at the same time have a positive impact by helping to solve a problem faced by the aerospace industry.
